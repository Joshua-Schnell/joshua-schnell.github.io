How did people in the Southern Maya Lowlands care for their mouths between 250 BCE and 1000 CE? This project draws on dental remains and the objects associated with oral practice to reconstruct everyday and specialist forms of care. The dissertation received the CLACS Dissertation Prize at Brown University.
