The Lab for Anthropological Bioarchaeology of the Maya (MayaLAB) is a research community based in the Department of Anthropology at the University of Pittsburgh, directed by Dr. Joshua Schnell. 

## Research

The MayaLAB is equipped with dental molding and casting facilities, a comparative faunal osteological collection, Dino-Lite digital microscopes and attachments for polarized light microscopy and transmitted light microscopy, an imaging PC and a suite of visualization and mapping software including Agisoft Metashape and Esri ArcGIS Pro, and a variety of equipment and space for experimental (bio)archaeology studies, including a Bambu Lab H2D Pro 3D printer. 

Many MayaLAB members also work closely with the MAIS Lab here at Pitt. The Mesoamerican Archaeology and Isotopic Sciences Lab, directed by Dr. Claire Ebert, provides training and facilities for isotope sample preparation.
